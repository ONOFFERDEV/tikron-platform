# Underpass timber redoubts, world Session 5

The existing opaque redoubt boxes now carry vertical timber revetment boards with
two horizontal walers. Their outer surface stays within 0.018 m of the authoritative
front/back face. Former side ventilation slits are now matching timber bands.
The full collision envelope remains solid; no artificial window is introduced.

The six former factory circulation stripe groups and four service thresholds now
read as flush duckboards. Each rectangle is clipped independently against actual
y=0 support boxes, so the excavated channel and descending ramps remain open.
These are thin visual surfaces, not a movement or material/footstep retune.
Objective outlines remain intact for legibility.

Seven existing material slots and the same 1024-square embedded AO image are reused.
The architecture was rebuilt with the standard 64-sample Blender bake and passes
the exact oriented-triangle audit. Collision and ground AO remain unchanged.
No light, render pass, new image, dependency, or paid generation was added.

Reproduction and byte receipt are in the marked world block of assets/README.md.
Evidence lives in .inspect/aaa-loop-world/session5-* and .inspect/world-s5-*.
Remaining period conversion debt includes office control panels, tall sluice frames,
low-cover industrial caps and repeated undamaged masonry. This increment does not
certify the complete map as an AAA environment.

## Session 6: trench use (arc 3/3)

Collision is unchanged: 219 boxes, eight ramps, ground AO bytes and all four
Undertow audits. Only render cladding and the atlas changed.

- The four long deployment walls lose their framed service bays and lamp strips.
  They now carry irregular vertical boards with some missing, stakes, two walers,
  corrugated-iron repair sheets, a two-course sandbag parapet and one gas-curtained
  dugout mouth per face. The mouth is a closed blanket on the solid face, not an opening.
- The 68 low covers, the yard/sluice benches (former switch consoles) and the
  redoubt tops become staggered burlap sandbag courses. Pump-hall consoles become
  plank map tables. The field office gets a string course, boarded windows, a notice
  board and a field telephone. The chimney is a brick stack with a corbel and soot.
- The dry drain's retaining walls are revetted with boards and stakes. A duckboard
  walk and gutter lie on the real -3 m floor and skip every pump baffle. Duckboard
  runs link both deployment trenches.
- The `pale` finish becomes burlap (0x8d8269). `undertow-surfaces.ts` gives it a
  procedural 0.62 x 0.30 m bag pattern with pillowed edges, which fades below pixel
  size. No texture, program slot or pass was added.
- Sluice frames outside z=0 become dark coursed masonry with plank gate leaves and
  iron straps. The pilot lamps shrink to lanterns but keep their phase colours,
  because those colours carry the signal state.
- The ground atlas gets warmer mud, trodden tracks with ruts along every B approach
  and both deployment routes, and standing water in the ruts.
